Phase 6 · the coupled climate

Every stage new on Earth (160K terrain cells), scored on Köppen against Beck's map per continent (each weighted equally), with every stage's own score as a guardrail. The rain now feeds back: clouds shade rainy land. The last few settings were tuned on Köppen within their literature ranges.
